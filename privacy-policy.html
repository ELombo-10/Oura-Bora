Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

⚠️ For personal use only — this application is operated by and for a single individual (the account owner) to view their own Oura Ring data. It is not a commercial product, not offered to or used by any other people, and accesses no one else’s data.

This Privacy Policy describes how the Lombardino Health Dashboard ("the App") handles information when you connect your Oura account.

Information we access

With your explicit authorization through Oura's OAuth2 consent screen, the App reads the following data from your own Oura account: sleep, readiness, activity, workout, session, and heart-rate metrics, plus basic personal info (age, biological sex, height, weight) where provided. The App only accesses the scopes you approve.

How we use it

Data is used solely to display your own health metrics within the dashboard. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

Storage

The App is for personal use by the account owner. Any cached data is stored locally and used only to render your dashboard. OAuth access tokens are stored securely and used only to make requests to the Oura API on your behalf.

Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access at any time from your Oura account settings at cloud.ouraring.com. Revoking access stops all further data retrieval.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to elil9@heavyconcepts.com.
